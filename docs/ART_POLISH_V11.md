# Art Polish V11

V11 is the first fidelity pass that upgrades the **individual reusable assets themselves**, not just the assembled proof.

## Upgraded assets

- **Fountain / angel**: richer basin stone shading, block joints, water ripples, relief panel, robe folds, face pixels, wing feather rows and water jets.
- **Market stalls**: denser shelf/cubby structure, many smaller produce/pottery silhouettes, fabric folds, scalloped awnings, hanging wares and side crates.
- **Round trees**: less circular canopy silhouettes, more clustered foliage, broken highlights, internal gaps, trunk/root detail and two silhouette variants.

## Composition

The proof stays on the 920x532 logical layout used by the reference-aligned v8-v10 passes. The fountain remains left of geometric center, stall/turret/banner positions follow the reference composition, and the scene continues to use the reusable tile/prop decomposition.

## Fidelity rule

No source pixels from `pix.png` are copied, cropped, masked or resampled into these assets. The reference is used only to judge proportions, placement, palette/value relationships and object detail.

## Remaining high-impact differences

1. The angel still needs more anatomical subtlety and a less schematic silhouette.
2. Tree canopy edges need even more irregularity and species variation.
3. Garden borders need more curved/stepped custom pieces beyond the current modular curb vocabulary.
4. Bottom-edge architecture still has less facade detail than the reference.
5. Far-left/far-right market clutter needs more unique prop silhouettes.
6. Final palette tuning should reduce the remaining flatness in some large green/stone regions.

V11 is therefore **closer but not final**. Further passes should continue modifying the individual PXO assets and then re-run Pixelorama validation.

# Authoritative visual reference: `pix.png`

`pix.png` in the repository root is the authoritative image for all future fidelity iterations. The earlier wide plaza screenshot used in chat is no longer the target composition.

## Reference facts

- Source: `pix.png`
- Size: **1254 x 1254 RGB**
- Rebuild working scale: **627 x 627 logical pixels**, exported 2x with nearest-neighbor to 1254 x 1254 for comparison.
- Rule remains unchanged: the reference may be measured/analyzed, but final `redraw/` pixels are authored from blank canvases; no crop/copy/resample of source pixels into final assets.

## Composition targets

1. Massive crystal tower on the vertical centerline, spanning the top/upper-middle district.
2. Fortified top wall with corner turrets and banners.
3. Dense upper residential/commercial districts: blue-roof cathedral/house cluster left, blue mansion and red inn right.
4. Open market plaza in the lower middle, with stalls, trees, lamps, banners, and angel fountain.
5. Main southern city wall and gate with twin blue-roof gate towers.
6. Bridge/road continuing down through water.
7. Harbor/docks at lower left and lower right, with ship on the left and waterwheel/waterfall details on the right.
8. Side canals/waterfalls, heavy vegetation, flowerbeds, crates/barrels and other edge clutter.
